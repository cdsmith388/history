# Whole-House Renovation Task List ## Rating Key **Skill level** * Basic: Cleaning, painting, simple hardware, covers, caulking * Intermediate: Flooring, trim, drywall repair, cabinetry, shelving * Advanced: Major carpentry, ceiling work, plumbing alterations * Licensed professional: Electrical, structural, or significant plumbing work **Effort** * Low: Under 2 hours * Medium: Half day to one day * High: Multiple days or substantial preparation * Very high: Major project involving several trades --- # First Floor ## Office ### Repair or replace crown molding * Materials: Crown molding, finish nails, construction adhesive, wood filler, caulk, primer, paint * Tools: Miter saw, nail gun or hammer, caulk gun * Skill level: Intermediate * Effort: Medium * Notes: Complete before final wall and ceiling touch-ups. ### Remove fluorescent fixtures and install replacement lighting * Materials: New light fixtures or LEDs, electrical boxes if needed, wire connectors, mounting hardware * Tools: Voltage tester, screwdrivers, wire strippers, ladder * Skill level: Licensed electrician recommended * Effort: Medium * Notes: Confirm whether the existing electrical boxes and wiring can support the replacements. ### Optional popcorn-ceiling removal * Materials: Plastic sheeting, painter’s tape, water sprayer, scraper, joint compound, primer, ceiling paint * Tools: Ceiling scraper, drywall knives, sander, respirator * Skill level: Intermediate * Effort: High * Notes: Test for asbestos before disturbing older popcorn texture. ### Install new flooring * Materials: Flooring, underlayment, transition strips, spacers, floor patch or leveling compound * Tools: Saw or flooring cutter, tapping block, pull bar * Skill level: Intermediate * Effort: High * Notes: Complete ceiling and major painting work first. ### Finish trim around the door * Materials: Door casing, finish nails, caulk, wood filler, primer, paint * Tools: Miter saw, nail gun or hammer * Skill level: Intermediate * Effort: Medium ### Repair messy paint around outlets * Materials: Spackle or joint compound, sandpaper, primer, matching wall paint * Tools: Putty knife, sanding block, small paintbrush * Skill level: Basic * Effort: Low * Notes: Remove outlet covers before repairing and repainting. ### Replace outlet and switch covers as needed * Materials: Matching outlet and switch plates * Tools: Screwdriver * Skill level: Basic * Effort: Low --- ##
